A school that grew with Mucheke

For decades, families in Mucheke and Rujeko have trusted us with their children's education.

Our students follow the ZIMSEC curriculum. They learn in groups, help each other and take their studies seriously. A visiting teacher from the United States once said she wished her own students valued learning as much as Mucheke students do.

Beyond the classroom, students play sport, join clubs and take part in the life of the city. We want every learner to leave with good results and good character.
