About Mucheke High

A government secondary school serving Mucheke, Rujeko and the wider city of Masvingo.

Welcome

Mucheke High School stands in Mucheke, the oldest township in Masvingo and home to the city's main bus rank and stadium.

We are a government day school run under the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education. Our students come from Mucheke, Rujeko, KMP, Runyararo and other parts of the city.

We follow the ZIMSEC curriculum from Form 1 to Form 6. Our teachers know their students by name, and many have given most of their working lives to this school.

Thank you for visiting our website. We hope it helps you get to know us.

Our values

These values guide how we teach, learn and treat each other.

Hard work

We do our best every day, in class and outside it.

Respect

We treat students, staff, parents and visitors with respect.

Honesty

We tell the truth and own our mistakes.

Unity

We help each other and learn together, the way our students work in groups.

Leadership

The school is led by a head appointed by the Ministry, supported by deputy heads, senior teachers and the School Development Committee (SDC).
