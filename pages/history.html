Our history

How Mucheke High grew with the township it is named after.

Named after Mucheke

Mucheke is the oldest township in Masvingo, the city once known as Fort Victoria. The Mucheke River runs close to the city centre, and the township, the stadium and our school all carry its name.

Mucheke High has served the families of this busy, high-density suburb for decades, giving thousands of young people a full secondary education close to home.

Former students and staff are invited to share photographs, memories and school records with the office to help preserve this history.
