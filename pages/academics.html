Forms 1 – 2

Junior secondary

Students build a firm base in English, Mathematics, Shona, Science, Heritage Studies and other core subjects. Teachers help new students settle in and form good study habits.

Forms 3 – 4

Ordinary Level

Students choose subjects for their ZIMSEC Ordinary Level exams. Science students do regular practicals. Extra lessons run in the weeks before exams.

Forms 5 – 6

Advanced Level

A Level students study three or four subjects in depth. They prepare for university, college and the world of work.

Life at Mucheke

Good habits. Steady progress.

Learning continues beyond each lesson. A regular study routine, careful revision and the confidence to ask questions help students make the most of secondary school.

Prepare

Bring the books and materials needed for the day. Review previous work before starting a new topic.

Practise

Work through examples, complete assignments and use teacher feedback to improve.

Ask for support

Speak to a subject teacher when a concept is unclear. Parents can arrange a conversation through the office.

Confirm current subject combinations and entry requirements with the school before making your choices.
