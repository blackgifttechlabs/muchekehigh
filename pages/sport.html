Sport

Sport teaches teamwork, discipline and pride in the school.

Every student can play

Students train after classes and compete against other Masvingo schools in zone, district and provincial events through NASH.

Each year the whole school takes part in inter-house athletics. The best athletes go on to represent Mucheke High.
