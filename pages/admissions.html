Admissions

How to join Mucheke High School in Form 1, Form 5 or another class.

How to apply

  1. Get the form

    Collect an application form from the school office or download it here.

  2. Fill it in

    Attach a copy of the birth certificate and the latest school report (Grade 7 results for Form 1, O Level results for Form 5).

  3. Hand it in

    Bring the form to the school office between 07:30 and 16:00, Monday to Friday.

  4. Wait for our reply

    We will call you to confirm a place and explain the next steps.
